## Trip Info

- **Transportation**: Private bus and domestic flights
- **Accommodation**: 3-star hotels
- **Departure from**: Seoul
- **Best season**: Spring and autumn
- **Tour type**: Eco-tour and hiking
- **Meals**: Confirmed in the final itinerary and quote
- **Language**: English and Korean
- **Fitness level**: Moderate — daily walking, some stairs and uneven terrain
- **Group size**: 5–25 guests

## Overview

Discover Korea's major destinations on a 12-day private journey. The route
connects Jeju Island, Suncheon, Boseong, Busan, Gyeongju, Sokcho, Seoul, the
DMZ, and Suwon with an English-speaking guide, private transportation, and
domestic flights where required.

## Highlights

- Jeju Island's volcanic landscapes and coastal scenery
- Boseong Green Tea Fields and Suncheon Bay
- Busan's markets, beaches, and coastal temples
- Gyeongju's UNESCO heritage sites
- Seoraksan National Park and Sokcho
- Seoul's historic palaces, markets, K-pop culture, and modern districts
- DMZ and Suwon Hwaseong Fortress

## Itinerary

- **Day 1**: Incheon Airport arrival
- **Day 2–3**: Jeju Island
- **Day 4**: Suncheon, Boseong, and transfer toward Busan
- **Day 5**: Busan
- **Day 6**: Gyeongju
- **Day 7**: Sokcho and Seoraksan
- **Day 8–9**: Seoul highlights
- **Day 10**: DMZ
- **Day 11**: Suwon
- **Day 12**: Incheon Airport departure

Flight schedules, hotel locations, and intercity transfer details are confirmed
before booking.

## FAQs

### Are domestic flights included?

Domestic flights required by the confirmed itinerary may be included. Each
flight and its baggage allowance will be listed in the final quote.

### Are hotels included?

Accommodation can be included for the 11-night itinerary. The hotel category,
locations, and room arrangements are confirmed before booking.

### Is transportation included?

Private transportation is arranged for the road sections, with domestic flights
used where appropriate. The exact vehicle and transfer plan depend on the group.

### Is the DMZ visit guaranteed?

No. DMZ access depends on military, weather, and government conditions. An
alternative activity may be suggested if the visit is cancelled.

### Is the itinerary fixed?

The major destinations define the journey, but sightseeing order and selected
stops can be adjusted after discussing your interests and pace.
