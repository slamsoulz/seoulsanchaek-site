No fixed route, no landmark checklist. Tell me what you're into — food,
history, shopping, a specific neighborhood, even a border-area trip like
the DMZ on the full-day tier — and I'll build the time around it with a
private car.

Every private car tour is custom by default — the course you have in mind
comes first. If it doesn't fit in the time we have, I'll tell you honestly
and suggest what to trim or swap. Drawing on experience coordinating
locations for international news and documentary crews, I move efficiently
and know how to adjust on the spot.

- **Included**: vehicle, guide, fuel/tolls/admission within the Seoul metro area (Seoul, Gimpo, Paju)
- **Not included**: personal meals, admission fees outside the metro area (noted separately)
- **Extending**: Additional time is $100 per hour.
- **Beyond the metro area** (Ganghwa, Yangpyeong, Gapyeong, etc.): Quote on request, depending on distance and operating costs.
- **Group size**: Prices are per private group, not per person. Groups of 4 or more require an inquiry for vehicle and operating arrangements.
- **Luggage note**: Please inquire with your group size and number of bags to confirm that the vehicle can accommodate everything.
- **Custom Korea Road Trip**: Explore popular destinations beyond Seoul, including Busan, Jeju, Jeonju, Daegu, Gyeongju, and Gangneung. Multi-city itineraries, including 3-city and 5-city trips, are available by inquiry only. Fuel, tolls, parking, vehicle arrangements, accommodation, and other operating costs are quoted separately for each itinerary.

## FAQs

### Is the price per person or per group?

Private car tour prices are charged per private group, not per person.

### Can groups of 4 or more join?

Yes, but groups of 4 or more require an inquiry so we can confirm the vehicle
and operating arrangements. The price may change.

### Can the vehicle carry a lot of luggage?

Luggage space is limited. Please send your group size and number of bags before
booking so we can confirm whether the vehicle is suitable.

### How much does it cost to extend the tour?

Additional time is $100 per hour, added on top of any tier.

### Can we choose our own itinerary?

Yes. Every private car tour is customized around your interests, preferred
areas, available time, and walking pace.

### Are trips outside Seoul available?

Yes. Custom Korea Road Trip itineraries to destinations such as Busan, Jeju,
Jeonju, Daegu, Gyeongju, and Gangneung are available by inquiry only.

### Are fuel, tolls, parking, and admission fees included?

Inclusions depend on the route and destination. Costs outside the standard
coverage are listed separately in your quote.
