Also known as a transit tour — same personal guiding, no vehicle. We get
around by subway and bus like a local would. A good option if you want a
private guide without the cost of a private car. Tell me what you're into
and I'll build the day around it — half day or full day, your call.

- **Included**: guide, on-site briefing
- **Not included**: personal meals, transit fares, admission fees
- **Group size**: 1 guest is $100; 2–9 guests are $70 per person. Groups of 10 or more require an inquiry.
- **Additional time**: After the 4-hour base, add $20 per person per hour for all groups. This applies to 5–7 hour and 8-hour tours.

## FAQs

### What places can we visit?

Depending on your interests and available time, the tour may include
Gyeongbokgung Palace, Gwanghwamun Square, Cheonggyecheon Stream, Myeongdong,
Dongdaemun Design Plaza, Hangang Parks, traditional markets, and local
neighborhoods.

### Is the itinerary fixed?

No. The route is adjusted around your interests, walking pace, preferred
neighborhoods, and available time.

### What happens if we want more than 4 hours?

Additional time is available at $20 per person per hour for all group sizes.

### Are transit fares and admission fees included?

Personal transit fares, meals, and admission fees are not included unless
specifically stated in your booking details.

### Is the tour suitable for children or guests with limited mobility?

Please tell us about mobility needs in advance. The route can often be adjusted,
but subway stations, stairs, and uneven streets may limit accessibility.

### Can groups of 10 or more join?

Yes, but groups of 10 or more require a separate inquiry and itinerary review.
