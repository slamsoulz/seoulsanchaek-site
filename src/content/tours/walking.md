Also known as a transit tour — same personal guiding, no vehicle. We get
around by subway and bus like a local would. A good option if you want a
private guide without the cost of a private car. Tell me what you're into
and I'll build the day around it — half day or full day, your call.

- **Included**: guide, on-site briefing
- **Not included**: personal meals, transit fares, admission fees
- **Group size**: 1 guest is $100; 2–9 guests are $70 per person. Groups of 10 or more require an inquiry.
- **Additional time**: After the 4-hour base, add $20 per person per hour for all groups. This applies to 5–7 hour and 8-hour tours.

## Example Half-Day Routes

These are examples, not fixed itineraries — I build the day around what you
want to see. Each runs four hours, on foot with subway and bus links.

**Route A — Palaces and old alleys**

- **0:00** Gwanghwamun Square. Start at Gwanghwamun Stn, Line 5
- **0:15** Gyeongbokgung Palace
- **1:35** Bukchon Hanok Village
- **2:25** Insadong
- **3:10** Ikseondong
- **3:40** Cheonggyecheon Stream, finishing at Jongno 3-ga Stn, Lines 1/3/5

All on foot, walking north to south. Gyeongbokgung closes on Tuesdays. The
guard-changing ceremony runs at 10:00 and 14:00, so a 09:45 or 13:45 start
catches it. Bukchon's residential lanes are open to visitors until 17:00 only.

**Route B — Haebangchon, Namsan and Myeongdong**

- **0:00** Noksapyeong Stn, Line 6. Village bus 02 up to Haebangchon
- **0:15** Sinheung Market and the back lanes
- **1:00** The 108 steps and the view back over the city
- **1:30** Up Sowol-ro into Namsan Park
- **2:00** Namsan Park and N Seoul Tower
- **3:00** Cable car down
- **3:20** Myeongdong, finishing at Myeongdong Stn, Line 4

The neighborhood tour buses skip. We ride up and walk down, so the climbing
is modest. Starting about two hours before sunset puts you on Namsan for the
view. The tower's observation deck is a separate ticket; the park and terrace
give you the city for free.

**Route C — Gangnam and the river**

- **0:00** Starfield Library and COEX. Start at Samseong Stn, Line 2
- **0:50** Bongeunsa Temple
- **1:50** Garosu-gil
- **3:00** Banpo Hangang Park, finishing at Sinbanpo Stn, Line 9

Modern Seoul, with a temple founded in 794 sitting among the office towers.
Less walking than the other two, more browsing.

## FAQs

### What places can we visit?

We won't visit all of these in one tour — based on your interests and
available time, we'll pick a handful from places like Gyeongbokgung Palace,
Gwanghwamun Square, Cheonggyecheon Stream, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun Design Plaza
(DDP), N Seoul Tower and Namsan Park, Bukchon Hanok Village, Insadong, COEX
Library, Bongeunsa, Gangnam, Hangang Parks, traditional markets, and local
neighborhoods. We try to reflect what you want as closely as possible, and
the guide fine-tunes the exact spots and order around the route and time
available.

### Is the itinerary fixed?

No. The route is adjusted around your interests, walking pace, preferred
neighborhoods, and available time.

### Can this be an evening tour?

Yes. Tell us you'd like an evening start and we'll shape the route around
food, night views, and nightlife-friendly neighborhoods like Gangnam,
Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and Myeongdong. The evening tour is a 4-hour base —
$120 for 1 guest, $85 per person for 2–9 guests — with optional additional
time, capped at about 5 hours so we wrap up by 10–11 PM.

### What happens if we want more than 4 hours?

Additional time is available at $20 per person per hour for all group sizes.

### Are transit fares and admission fees included?

Personal transit fares, meals, and admission fees are not included unless
specifically stated in your booking details.

### Is the tour suitable for children or guests with limited mobility?

Please tell us about mobility needs in advance. The route can often be adjusted,
but subway stations, stairs, and uneven streets may limit accessibility.

### Can groups of 10 or more join?

Yes, but groups of 10 or more require a separate inquiry and itinerary review.
