Straightforward one-way pickup or drop-off between Incheon/Gimpo Airport
and your hotel. No touring included — just a dependable ride with a driver
who speaks English. The vehicle can usually accommodate up to 3 guests, but
the number of bags may make 3-person transfers unavailable. Please share your
group size and luggage count to confirm.

**Pricing by drop-off/pickup area**

| Zone | Covers | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul | All of Seoul, including Myeongdong, Gangnam | $60 (approx. ₩82,000) |
| Metro Area | Ilsan, Bundang, Gimpo, Incheon city, and similar nearby areas | $70 (approx. ₩96,000) |
| Other | Suwon, Yongin, Uijeongbu, Paju, and further out | Quote on request |

"Other" destinations vary too much in round-trip distance and time to flat-rate
fairly — tell me where you're headed and I'll quote it based on real
fuel, tolls, and drive time.

- **Included**: driver, vehicle, one-way transfer
- **Extra guests**: small surcharge
- **Luggage note**: 3 guests may not be possible with a large amount of luggage. Please inquire with your group size and number of bags.

## FAQs

### How many guests can the vehicle take?

The vehicle can usually take up to 3 guests, depending on the amount and size
of the luggage. Please share both your group size and bag count to confirm.

### Is this a tour?

No. This is a one-way airport pickup or drop-off service. Sightseeing is not
included.

### Where will we meet at the airport?

Meeting instructions will be confirmed before the pickup, including the
terminal, arrival details, and contact method.

### Can you pick us up at Gimpo Airport?

Yes. Gimpo Airport transfers may be available. Please include the airport,
flight details, destination, and luggage count in your inquiry.

### What if we have too much luggage for 3 guests?

Please inquire before booking. A different vehicle or arrangement may be
needed, and the price may change.

### Are tolls and fuel included?

The quoted transfer price is based on the pickup area, destination, route, and
operating conditions. Any exceptions will be explained in the quote.
