## Overview

A private full-day Seoul experience for travelers who want to see the city by
metro and on foot. The route can include palaces, markets, modern landmarks,
and neighborhoods such as Gyeongbokgung, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun, and Gangnam.

## Highlights

- Gyeongbokgung Palace and Gwanghwamun Square
- Cheonggyecheon Stream and Myeongdong
- Dongdaemun Design Plaza
- N Seoul Tower and Namsan Park
- Bukchon Hanok Village, Insadong, COEX Library, Bongeunsa, and Gangnam

## FAQs

### Is this a private tour?

Yes. The tour is arranged for your group, with no unrelated guests added.

### How long does it take?

It is a full-day tour. The exact start and finish time are coordinated around
your arrival, departure, and preferred itinerary.

### Can we change the sights?

Yes. The route can be adjusted according to your available time and interests.

### Is luggage storage available at the airport?

Airport luggage storage may be available at an additional cost. Please ask in
advance if you need this service.

### Are the subway stations wheelchair accessible?

Accessibility varies by station and attraction. Please share mobility needs
before booking so the route can be reviewed.
