## Trip Info

- **Transportation**: Metro and walking
- **Departure from**: Seoul or agreed pickup point
- **Tour type**: City tour and evening walk
- **Language**: English
- **Fitness level**: Easy to moderate
- **Group size**: 2–15 guests

## Overview

A flexible evening tour through Seoul's food, culture, shopping, and nightlife
districts. The route may include Bongeunsa Temple, Starfield Library, Gangnam,
Dongdaemun Design Plaza, and Myeongdong.

## Highlights

- Bongeunsa Temple and COEX Starfield Library
- Gangnam after dark
- Dongdaemun Design Plaza
- Myeongdong food and shopping streets

## Cost

This tour is available by inquiry. The final quote depends on duration, group
size, meeting point, transportation, meals, and any admission fees.

## FAQs

### What time does the tour start?

The start time is flexible and is arranged around your preferred evening plan.

### Can we choose the neighborhoods?

Yes. Tell us whether you prefer food, shopping, architecture, K-pop culture, or
night views and we will shape the route accordingly.

### Is dinner included?

Meals are not automatically included. Restaurants and food stops can be added
to the itinerary and confirmed in the quote.

### Is transportation included?

Metro and walking are the standard options. Any additional transportation is
confirmed in advance.
