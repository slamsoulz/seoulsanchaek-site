## Overview

This 7-day flexible tour covers Seoul, the DMZ, Suwon, Gyeongju, and Busan.
It combines cultural landmarks, UNESCO heritage, coastal scenery, and local
insights with private transportation and an English-speaking guide.

## Highlights

- Seoul palaces, markets, modern culture, and K-pop energy
- DMZ and N Seoul Tower
- Suwon Hwaseong Fortress
- Gyeongju, the historic capital of the Silla Dynasty
- Busan's coastal temples, markets, and hillside villages

## Itinerary

- **Day 1**: Arrival and Seoul orientation
- **Day 2**: Seoul cultural highlights
- **Day 3**: DMZ tour, subject to government and operational conditions
- **Day 4**: Suwon Hwaseong Fortress and Starfield Suwon
- **Day 5**: Gyeongju heritage sites
- **Day 6**: Busan coastal and cultural highlights
- **Day 7**: Departure from Incheon or Gimhae Airport

The final route and intercity transfers are confirmed before booking.

## FAQs

### Is this a private or group tour?

It is a private tour for your group only. Vehicle size and operating details
depend on the final group size.

### Is the DMZ visit guaranteed?

No. The DMZ is subject to military, weather, and government operating
conditions. An alternative may be offered if access is unavailable.

### Are airport transfers included?

Airport pickup and departure transfer can be included. Confirm the airport,
flight details, and luggage count in your inquiry.

### Can the itinerary be customized?

Yes. The route can be adjusted around your interests and available time, within
the practical limits of a 7-day intercity journey.
